# Qbikk headline candidates

1. **Your right hand. In your pocket.** — recommended hero: everyday support in a portable form.
2. **Be there. Keep the details.** — short, presence-focused alternative.
3. **A little backup for your everyday.** — warm and approachable.
4. **Small device. Less on your mind.** — compact hardware, practical benefit.
5. **Keep the conversation. Carry on with your day.** — captures the use and outcome.
6. **Your everyday secretary, wherever life takes you.** — explicit positioning.
7. **Turn everyday conversations into your next steps.** — clear value proposition.
8. **Listen now. Find the important parts later.** — plain explanation of recording and recall.

Suggested subheadline:
Meet Qbikk: a pocket AI recorder in development, designed to turn conversations,
calls, and meetings into transcripts, summaries, and next steps.

These describe the intended product direction. Do not add claims about accuracy,
battery life, pricing, integrations, or release dates until confirmed.
